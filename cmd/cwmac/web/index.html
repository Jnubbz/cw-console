SWR sweep

    
Short low-power CW carriers across the band, reading the radio's SWR meter at each step. It uses the CW key line, so Transmit must be on (DTR or RTS). The radio goes back exactly as it was. Esc stops it. Only sweep where your license lets you transmit, and listen first. It starts on the band the radio is on. With a tuner in line it measures the tuner's match, not the antenna (an auto-tuner may try to retune on every step): bypass the tuner to see the antenna itself, or sweep just Around dial.
